LUFAN
YOUR LUDO WORLD · VERSION 1.0

Play together. Race to win.

LUFAN is a Classic Ludo game designed to bring friends and family together through fun, strategy, and friendly competition.

  • Classic Ludo-style gameplay
  • 2, 3, and 4 player modes
  • Token colour selection
  • Room creation and room-code interface

Room features shown in this version do not by themselves guarantee live cross-device multiplayer; that requires a connected real-time backend.

Rules & Fair Play

  • Take turns and follow the dice and legal-move prompts shown in the game.
  • Use the available token controls only for legal moves; the game’s implemented rules and prompts determine valid actions.
  • Play fairly, respect other players, and do not intentionally disrupt a match.

Privacy

Use a nickname and avoid entering passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information. This game version uses browser storage for some local settings and room-interface state. Information stored only in one browser is not automatically shared with another device.

If the game is later connected to an online server, its data handling should be reviewed against that server’s actual privacy policy before use.
